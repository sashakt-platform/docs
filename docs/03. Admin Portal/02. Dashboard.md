# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It gives you an at-a-glance view of activity across your organization.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Dashboard with stat cards_

## What's on the Dashboard

Depending on your role, you'll see cards summarizing:

- **Questions** — Total questions in the bank
- **Tests** — Total tests created
- **Users** — Admin and staff accounts in your org
- **Submissions** — Total test submissions, often filterable by date

Each card is clickable — selecting a card jumps you to the full list view for that resource.

## Filters

Most dashboards include filters at the top to scope what you see:

- **Date range** — Last 7 days, last 30 days, custom range
- **State / District** (if your role spans multiple)
- **Test or test category**

Filters update every card on the page in real time.

:::info Scoped views
If you're a State Admin — or a Test Admin scoped to a state or district — the Dashboard automatically scopes to your region. You won't see stats from other regions.
:::
