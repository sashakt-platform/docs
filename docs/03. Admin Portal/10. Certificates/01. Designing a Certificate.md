# Designing a Certificate

A **certificate** is a document Sashakt issues to candidates after they finish (or pass) a test. You design it once and Sashakt fills in candidate-specific details automatically.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Certificate designer_

## Starting from a template

Open **Certificates** and click **Create Certificate**. Pick a starting template or begin from scratch.

You'll get a canvas with:

- **Background image** — Upload your own design (PDF, PNG, or JPG)
- **Text blocks** — Drag them anywhere on the canvas
- **Tokens** — Insert placeholders for candidate name, score, date, etc. See [Tokens & Variables](./tokens-and-variables).
- **Signatures and logos** — Upload images and position them

## Tips for a good certificate

- **Use a high-resolution background** — 300 DPI prints crisply.
- **Leave white space** — Don't crowd the canvas with text blocks.
- **Preview with long names** — A test name like "_Advanced Foundations of Mathematical Reasoning_" needs more room than _"Math Quiz_".
- **Test the printable size** — A4 and Letter render slightly differently.

## Previewing

Click **Preview** to see the certificate with sample data. Use a mix of short and long sample names to catch overflow issues early.

## Attaching to a test

Once your certificate is saved, attach it to a test in that test's settings. Sashakt issues a certificate automatically to every candidate who meets the test's passing criteria.

## Re-issuing

If a certificate template changes after issuance, existing certificates do **not** update automatically. To re-issue with the new design, open the candidate's submission and click **Re-issue Certificate**.
