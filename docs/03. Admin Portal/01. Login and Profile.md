# Login & Profile

Everything you do in Sashakt starts with signing in. This page covers logging in, changing your password, and updating your profile.

## Signing in

Open your Portal URL in a browser — it'll look like `https://portal.sashakt.in/<your-organization-shortcode>` — and enter your email and password.

![Login screen](/img/screenshots/login.png)

You can update your password at any time from the **Profile** page. Pick something strong — your account controls real tests and candidate data.

:::tip Forgot your password?
Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Sashakt will email you a reset link. If the email doesn't arrive, ask your System Admin to trigger a manual reset.
:::

## Updating your profile

Click your name in the bottom-left corner of the navigation to open **My Profile**.

![Profile page](/img/screenshots/profile.png)

From here you can update:

- Your name, email, and phone number
- Your password (under **My Password**)

Your **role** is displayed in the **Role** field on the profile page but cannot be changed by you — only a System Admin can change roles.

## Signing out

Click **Logout** at the bottom of the left navigation. Sashakt sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity for security.
