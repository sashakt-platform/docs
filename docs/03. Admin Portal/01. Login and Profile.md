# Login & Profile

Everything you do in Sashakt starts with signing in. This page covers logging in, changing your password, and updating your profile.

## Signing in

Open your Portal URL in a browser — it'll look like `https://portal.sashakt.in/<your-organization-shortcode>` — and enter your email and password.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Portal login screen_

After your first login, Sashakt asks you to set a new password. Pick something strong — your account controls real tests and candidate data.

:::tip Forgot your password?
Click **Forgot password?** on the login screen. Sashakt will email you a reset link. If the email doesn't arrive, ask your System Admin to trigger a manual reset.
:::

## Updating your profile

Click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Profile**.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Profile page_

From here you can update:

- Your name and contact details
- Your password

Your **role** is shown at the top of the profile page but cannot be changed by you — only a System Admin can change roles.

## Signing out

Click your name in the top-right corner and choose **Sign Out**. Sashakt sessions expire automatically after a period of inactivity for security.
