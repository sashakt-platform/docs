# Users & Permissions

The **Users** section is where you add the people who run tests for your organization. Sashakt's permission model is role-driven — assigning the right role is the main lever you have.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Users list_

## Adding a user

1. Open **Users** from the left navigation.
2. Click **Add User**.
3. Fill in name, email, and phone.
4. Choose a **role** (see [User Roles](../getting-started/user-roles) for what each role can do).
5. If the role is **State Admin** or **District Admin**, pick the entity (state or district) the user is scoped to.
6. Click **Save**.

The new user receives an email with a temporary password and a link to the Portal.

## Editing or deactivating a user

Click any user row to open their profile. From there you can:

- Update their name, email, phone, or role
- Reset their password (sends them a new email)
- **Deactivate** the account — keeps history intact but blocks login

:::tip Deactivate, don't delete
Deactivating preserves the user's name on the tests and submissions they touched. Outright deletion is only available in special circumstances — usually for compliance.
:::

## Filtering and searching

The Users list supports filters by role, state, district, and status. Use these when your org grows large enough that scrolling becomes painful.

## How permissions actually work

Sashakt enforces permissions on the **server side**, not just in the UI. A user can never see or change data outside their role's scope, even if they try to manipulate URLs. You don't need to worry about hidden permissions leaking — what you see in [User Roles](../getting-started/user-roles) is exactly what each role can touch.
