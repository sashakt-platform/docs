# Media in Questions

You can enrich any question with media — **images** uploaded directly to Sashakt, and **audio** or **video** referenced by URL. This is essential for diagrams, listening comprehension, video explanations, photo identification, and a lot more.

![Question editor with an image attached](/img/screenshots/question-editor-media.png)

## Adding an image

Images are uploaded directly into Sashakt.

1. Open a question for editing.
2. Click **Add Image** in the question editor.
3. Either drag a file in or click to choose one.
4. Save.

The image appears above the question text in the candidate view.

**Supported formats:** PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP.

**Size limits:** Keep images under 1 MB where possible. Large files slow down low-bandwidth candidates.

:::tip Use clear, compressed images
Aim for ~150 KB per image. Diagrams render best as PNG, photographs as JPG. Test on a phone before publishing.
:::

## Adding audio or video

Audio and video are not uploaded to Sashakt — you host them somewhere your candidates can reach (YouTube, Vimeo, your own CDN, Google Drive with public access, etc.) and paste the **URL** into the question.

1. Open a question for editing.
2. Click **Add Audio** or **Add Video**.
3. Paste the URL of the file or video.
4. Save.

Candidates see a player above the question text and can play the clip during their attempt.

## Choosing a host for audio and video

A few things to verify before you paste a URL:

- **Publicly accessible** — The link must work without login. If it asks for a password, candidates can't see it.
- **Direct streaming** — Sites that embed properly (YouTube, Vimeo) are the easiest path. For raw audio/video files, the URL should serve the file directly with the right MIME type.
- **Stable** — The URL shouldn't expire. Signed URLs from object stores often do — prefer a permanent link.
- **Bandwidth-friendly** — Lower bitrates render well on field connections. A 320 kbps audio clip or 360p video is usually plenty.

:::caution Test before publishing
Open the test on a phone (and ideally on a slow connection) before sharing the link with candidates. A URL that loads on your office Wi-Fi can stall on a 2G connection.
:::

## Where media is stored

- **Images** live in your organization's connected Google Cloud Storage bucket (see [Google Cloud Storage](../../integrations/google-cloud-storage)).
- **Audio and video** stay on whichever service you linked. Sashakt only stores the URL — not the file itself.
