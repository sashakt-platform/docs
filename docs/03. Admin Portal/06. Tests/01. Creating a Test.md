# Creating a Test

A **test** is the thing your candidates actually take. Building one is mostly about picking questions and deciding the rules.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: New test wizard_

## Step 1 — Basics

Open **Tests** and click **Create Test**. Give it:

- A clear **name** — candidates will see this
- An optional **description**
- One or more **tags** (e.g. _Mathematics_, _Class 8_, _2024 Batch_)

## Step 2 — Choose questions

You can pick questions in three ways:

1. **One by one** — Browse the question bank, click to add each.
2. **By tag** — "Add 20 random questions tagged _Class 8_ + _Mathematics_."
3. **Bulk paste** — Paste a list of question IDs from a spreadsheet.

Sashakt shows a live count of how many questions you've added and their total marks.

## Step 3 — Configure sections (optional)

If your test has logical parts — say, _Reading_, _Math_, _Logical Reasoning_ — group them into [sections](./sections-and-question-selection). Each section can have its own timer and shuffling rules.

## Step 4 — Set the rules

Configure how the test behaves: timer, scoring, OMR mode, feedback, and more. See [Test Settings](./test-settings) for the full reference.

## Step 5 — Attach a form

Pick the registration [form](../forms/form-builder) candidates will fill in before they start the test. If you don't pick one, candidates can start with just a name.

## Step 6 — Publish and share

Click **Publish**. Sashakt generates a unique link. Share that link with candidates — that's all they need.
