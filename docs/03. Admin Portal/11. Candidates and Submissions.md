# Candidates & Submissions

Once candidates start taking your tests, their attempts show up here. This is where you review who took what, how they did, and download data for analysis.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Submissions list_

## What a submission contains

Each submission row represents one candidate's attempt at one test. It includes:

- Candidate's registration form responses (name, location, etc.)
- Start time, end time, and duration
- Status — _In progress_, _Submitted_, _Auto-submitted_, _Abandoned_
- Score (raw and percentage)
- Certificate, if one was issued

Click a row to see the full attempt — every question with the candidate's answer and the correct answer.

## Filtering submissions

The list supports filters for:

- **Test** — Show only attempts at a specific test
- **Date range**
- **Status**
- **State / District** (if your role is scoped that way)
- **Score threshold** — e.g. only show candidates who scored above 80%

Use filters to build the slice you actually care about, then export.

## Exporting

Click **Export** at the top of the filtered list. Sashakt produces a CSV with one row per candidate, including their form responses and final score.

:::tip Need more advanced reporting?
For larger datasets and dashboards, connect [BigQuery Sync](../integrations/bigquery-sync). Sashakt pushes submissions to BigQuery automatically, where you can join, slice, and visualize with the tools your team already uses.
:::

## In-progress submissions

A test attempt that hasn't been submitted yet shows up as _In progress_. Sashakt auto-submits the attempt when the timer expires. If a candidate closes their browser mid-test, their answers up to that point are preserved.
