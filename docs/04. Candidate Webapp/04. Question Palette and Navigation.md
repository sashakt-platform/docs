# Question Palette & Navigation

The **Question Palette** is the panel on the right side of a test that shows every question at a glance. It's how candidates skip around without losing their place.

![Test with question palette](/img/screenshots/webapp-test-taking.png)

## Reading the palette

Each square in the palette represents one question. The colours and indicators tell you the status:

- **Unanswered** — Visited but no answer yet
- **Answered** — Answered
- **Mandatory** — Marked with an asterisk; must be answered before submitting
- **Marked for Review** — Flagged by the candidate to come back to

Candidates can tap any square to jump straight to that question.

## Navigation buttons

At the bottom of every question card:

- **Previous** — Go back one question
- **Next** — Move forward

Between them, Sashakt shows the current page and a running count — e.g. `Page 1 of 3 | 1–1 of 3 Questions`.

## Mark for Review

Each question card has a **Mark for review** button. Marked questions show up flagged in the palette, making them easy to find before submitting.

A common strategy: answer the easy questions first, mark anything you're unsure about, and come back via the palette.

## Clear answer

Each question card has a **Clear answer** button that resets the candidate's response for that question.

## Marks display

Each question shows its **marks** ("Marks: +1") if the test admin has turned on _Show marks to candidate during test_ in [Test Rules](../admin-portal/tests/test-settings#test-rules).

## Instructions

The **Instructions** button in the top bar reopens the test's [pre-test guidelines](../admin-portal/tests/test-settings#pre-test-guidelines) at any time during the test.

## Question Palette and Mark for Review are optional

Both features are controlled by [Organisation Settings](../admin-portal/organization-settings) — your organisation may have either turned off, in which case candidates won't see them.
