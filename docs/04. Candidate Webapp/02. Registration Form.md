# Registration Form

Before a candidate sees the first question, they fill in a registration form. This is how Sashakt knows who is taking the test.

> _📷 Screenshot placeholder: Registration form_

## What candidates fill in

The exact fields depend on what an admin built in the [Form Builder](../admin-portal/forms/form-builder). Common fields include:

- Name
- Phone or email
- Date of birth
- State, district, school (using a location picker)
- Class / grade
- A unique ID (employee ID, student ID, etc.)

## Required vs optional

Required fields are marked with a red asterisk. Candidates can't submit the form until all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Validation

Sashakt validates fields in real time. The candidate sees error messages right under the field as they type — wrong email format, missing required field, invalid date.

## Saving progress

If a candidate refreshes the page mid-form, their answers so far are preserved. They can pick up where they left off.

## What happens after submitting

After the candidate clicks **Continue** at the end of the form, Sashakt:

1. Saves their registration data
2. Moves them to the next screen — either the [mode selector](./omr-vs-question-mode) or directly into the test

Once a candidate has registered for a test, their progress is tied to their browser session. Returning to the test link later in the same browser resumes the same attempt.
