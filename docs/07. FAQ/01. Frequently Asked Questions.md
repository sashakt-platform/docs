# Frequently Asked Questions

A running list of questions Sashakt admins and candidates ask most often. If yours isn't here, ask your System Admin or open an issue on the [docs repo](https://github.com/sashakt-platform/docs).

## General

### What is Sashakt?

Sashakt is an open-source assessment platform that makes it effortless for organizations to create, customize, and conduct assessments at scale. It's built by [Project Tech4Dev](https://projecttech4dev.org) in collaboration with Veddis Foundation and Avanti Fellows. See [Sashakt Overview](/).

### Is Sashakt free?

Sashakt is open source. You can self-host it at no licensing cost. If you'd like a managed deployment, get in touch via the [Sashakt page](https://projecttech4dev.org/sashakt).

### What languages does Sashakt support?

The candidate webapp ships with English and Hindi. Admin documentation is currently in English.

## Tests

### Can a candidate take a test more than once?

By default, no — one attempt per candidate. Admins can enable multiple attempts per test in [Test Settings](./admin-portal/tests/test-settings).

### What happens if a candidate loses internet mid-test?

Their answers up to that point are saved on the server. They can return to the test link from the same browser and resume.

### Can I extend the timer mid-test?

No. Timers are fixed once a test starts. Plan the duration carefully before publishing.

### Can I edit a question while a test is live?

Yes, but be careful — see the warning in [Creating Questions](./admin-portal/question-bank/creating-questions). Changing a correct answer mid-test creates unfair scoring for candidates who already attempted.

## Certificates

### Where do I design the certificate?

In **Google Slides**. You design the layout there, add token placeholders like `{{candidate_name}}`, share the slide, and paste the URL into Sashakt. See [Designing a Certificate](./admin-portal/certificates/designing-a-certificate).

### Can I re-issue a certificate after I update the template?

Yes — open the candidate's submission and click **Re-issue Certificate**. Existing certificates do not update automatically when you edit the Google Slide.

## Roles and permissions

### Why can't I see the Users page?

Your role doesn't have permission to manage users. State Admins and Test Admins typically don't. See [User Roles](./getting-started/user-roles).

### Can I create a custom role?

Roles are fixed at the platform level. If you need a permission combination that isn't covered, talk to your System Admin about whether one of the existing roles fits — or open a feature request.

## Data and integrations

### Where is candidate data stored?

In your organization's database, alongside the rest of your Sashakt data. Self-hosted deployments keep this entirely under your control.

### Can I export everything as CSV?

Yes. Submissions, form responses, and the question bank can all be exported. For ongoing analytics, set up [BigQuery Sync](./integrations/bigquery-sync).

### How do I back up Sashakt?

Sashakt's data lives in PostgreSQL and your GCS bucket. Standard backup tooling for both (managed Postgres snapshots, GCS lifecycle/versioning) is the right approach. Your DevOps team should set this up at deploy time.

## Troubleshooting

### Candidates report they didn't receive email

Check your SMTP configuration and DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). See [Email (SMTP)](./integrations/email-smtp).

### A bulk question upload failed

Open the validation preview — Sashakt highlights problem rows with reasons. Fix them in the CSV and re-upload. See [Bulk Upload](./admin-portal/question-bank/bulk-upload).

### The candidate webapp is showing an old version of the test

Ask the candidate to hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R). Sashakt deploys are usually picked up automatically, but cached browsers can lag.
