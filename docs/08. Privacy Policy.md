# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information Sashakt collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. It applies to the Sashakt Portal (the admin app), the Sashakt Webapp (the candidate app), and Sashakt Core (the backend API and database). See the [Sashakt Overview](/) for how these fit together.

## Who we are

Sashakt is an open-source assessment platform built by [Project Tech4Dev](https://projecttech4dev.org) (Tech4Dev). Sashakt can be used in two ways:

- **Managed deployment:** Tech4Dev hosts and operates Sashakt for your organization.
- **Self-hosted deployment:** your organization runs Sashakt on its own infrastructure.

Throughout this policy, **organization** means the NGO, government body, or training program that uses Sashakt to run assessments. **Admins** are the people who manage Sashakt for an organization. **Candidates** are the people who take tests.

For managed deployments, the organization decides what data is collected from its candidates and why. Tech4Dev processes that data only to run the service on the organization's behalf. For self-hosted deployments, this policy describes how the software behaves, but your organization is solely responsible for the data it stores and for how it hosts, secures, and shares it.

## What we collect

### Admin accounts

- Name, email address, and phone number
- Password (stored in hashed form, never in plain text)
- Role and organization
- Organization settings configured by admins

### Candidate data

What candidates provide depends on the [registration form](./candidate-webapp/registration-form) the organization attached to the test. Common fields include name, phone or email, location (state, district, block, school), class or grade, and a unique ID such as a student or employee ID. Organizations should collect only what they need.

### Assessment data

- Answers, selected options, and question progress
- Start and submission times, scores, and results
- Certificates generated for candidates

### Content created by admins

Questions, tests, tags, entities, forms, certificate templates, and media files (images, audio, and video) uploaded by admins.

### Integration credentials

If an organization connects BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, or Google Slides, the Google service account key it provides is stored by Sashakt. See [Integrations](./integrations).

### Technical information

Information needed to keep a session active and the service running, such as session identifiers and basic server logs.

### Support

When you contact us for support, we may collect the details you share with us.

## How we use information

- Running assessments: registering candidates, delivering tests, saving answers, scoring, and showing results
- Issuing certificates to candidates
- Letting admins manage users, questions, tests, and reports
- Syncing assessment data to the organization's own data warehouse for analysis
- Providing technical and operational support
- Improving the reliability and usability of the platform
- Sending service-related communication, such as password reset emails

We do not sell personal information.

Organizations may analyze candidate data for their own reporting, research, and program evaluation. Tech4Dev has no role in that analysis. Organizations are responsible for how they use candidate data outside Sashakt.

## Cookies and browser storage

Sashakt uses cookies or similar browser storage only to keep you signed in (admins) and to tie a candidate's progress to their browser session so they can resume a test. We do not use them for advertising.

## Third-party services

Sashakt connects to the following Google Cloud services, each configured per organization using the organization's own Google service account:

| Service | What it does with data |
|---------|------------------------|
| **BigQuery** | Receives submissions, responses, tests, and questions synced from Sashakt into the organization's own BigQuery project, where the organization analyzes them. |
| **Google Cloud Storage** | Stores media files attached to questions and tests. Files are served to candidates through time-limited signed URLs. |
| **Google Slides** | Renders certificates from the organization's template and exports them as PDFs. Candidate details used on a certificate (for example, the candidate's name) are inserted into the template. |

Data held in these services is subject to the organization's own Google Cloud configuration and to Google's terms and privacy policies. We do not share personal information with any other third parties for their own purposes.

## How we store information

- **Candidate and assessment data** is stored in the Sashakt database, along with the rest of the organization's data. Media files are stored in the organization's Cloud Storage bucket.
- **Isolation:** each organization's data is kept separate. In BigQuery, Sashakt appends an organization-specific suffix to the dataset name so that organizations sharing a Google Cloud project do not share data.
- **Retention:** we keep data for as long as the organization uses Sashakt, or until the organization asks us to delete it. Organizations decide how long to keep candidate data, and should delete what they no longer need.
- **Backups:** the database and storage bucket may be backed up for recovery. Deleted data may persist in backups until those backups expire.

## How we secure information

- Data in transit is protected with HTTPS.
- Integration credentials (service account keys) are encrypted at rest and are never returned to the browser.
- Access is role-based. Admins see only what their role and region permit. See [User Roles](./getting-started/user-roles).
- Passwords are stored hashed, and admin sessions expire after a period of inactivity.
- Candidate media is served using signed URLs that expire.

No system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access, hacking, loss, or breaches will never occur. If you think your account has been compromised, change your password and email [info@projecttech4dev.org](mailto:info@projecttech4dev.org) right away.

## Data privacy and access by Tech4Dev

Tech4Dev does not access an organization's candidate data except where needed to operate the service, to resolve a problem the organization has reported, or where required by law. Where access is needed for debugging or support, we will do it only with the organization's knowledge and permission.

## When we might share information

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, including court orders and requests from government or law enforcement agencies. Where the law allows, we will try to tell the organization beforehand.

## Where data is processed

Data is processed and stored where the Sashakt instance is deployed. For self-hosted deployments, that is the infrastructure your organization chooses. If your organization has data-location requirements, raise them with us before a managed deployment is set up.

## Your rights and choices

- **Access and correction:** admins can update their profile from the Portal. Candidates and admins can ask their organization, or us, what data we hold about them and ask for corrections.
- **Deletion:** you can request permanent deletion of your organization's data and user data by emailing [info@projecttech4dev.org](mailto:info@projecttech4dev.org). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, so export anything you need first. See the [FAQ](./faq).
- **Candidates:** if you took a test and want your data removed or corrected, contact the organization that ran the test. They control that data and can ask us to act on it.

We will aim to respond to requests within a reasonable time.

## Children's data

Organizations such as schools may use Sashakt to assess minors. In that case, the organization is responsible for obtaining any parental or guardian consent required by applicable law before collecting a child's data through Sashakt.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in these docs.

## Frequently asked questions

**What happens if there is a data breach?**
If a breach affects a managed deployment, we will promptly inform the affected organization so it can meet its own obligations to candidates.

**What happens to our data if we stop using Sashakt?**
Your data stays with your organization, including anything synced to your own BigQuery project. Once you confirm that you are ending the association, you can ask us to permanently delete your organization's Sashakt data.

**Can Sashakt see the data in our BigQuery project?**
Sashakt writes to the BigQuery project you configure using the service account you provide. You control that account's permissions and can revoke it at any time.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct, or delete data, can be sent to [info@projecttech4dev.org](mailto:info@projecttech4dev.org).
