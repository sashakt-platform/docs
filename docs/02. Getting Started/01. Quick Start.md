# Quick Start

This guide gets you from "I have a Sashakt account" to "I'm ready to build my first test" in about five minutes.

## Before you begin

You'll need:

- The **URL of your Sashakt Portal** — `https://portal.sashakt.in/<your-organization-shortcode>` (your System Admin will share the exact link)
- **Login credentials** from the person who set up your organization
- A modern browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

:::info Don't have an account yet?
If your organization is already using Sashakt, ask your System Admin to create an account for you. If you're setting up a brand-new organization, reach out to the Sashakt team.
:::

## Step 1 — Log in to the Portal

Open your organization's Portal URL in your browser — it'll look like `https://portal.sashakt.in/your-org-shortcode`. You'll see the login screen.

![Login screen](/img/screenshots/login.png)

Enter the email address and password you were given, then click **Sign In**.


## Step 2 — Find your way around

Once you're logged in, the **left-hand navigation** is where you spend most of your time.

![Portal navigation](/img/screenshots/tests-list.png)

Depending on your role, you'll see some or all of these sections:

- **Tests** — Create and manage tests
- **Test Templates** — Reusable test configurations
- **Question Bank** — Add and organize questions
- **Tag Management** — Categorize tests and questions
- **Forms** — Build registration forms for candidates
- **Certificates** — Manage certificates issued to candidates
- **Entities** — Manage locations, schools, or other org structures
- **Users** — Manage who can access the Portal
- **My Organisation** — Organisation Details and Settings (System Admins only)

Don't worry if some sections aren't visible — that's expected and depends on your [user role](./user-roles).

## Step 3 — Try a small test run

The fastest way to feel comfortable is to make something throwaway.

1. Go to **Question Bank** and create **two or three sample questions**.
2. Go to **Tests** and click **Create Test**.
3. Give it a name like _"My first test"_, pick the questions you just added, and save.
4. Copy the test's link in a new browser tab to see the candidate experience in the Webapp.

That's the whole loop. Everything else in Sashakt is a richer version of those three steps — better question types, smarter test settings, cleaner forms, prettier certificates.
