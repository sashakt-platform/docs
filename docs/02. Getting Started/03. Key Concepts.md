# Key Concepts

This page is a glossary you can come back to whenever a word in Sashakt doesn't quite mean what you expected. Most of the platform is built around these eight ideas.

## Organization

An **organization** is the top-level container for everything else. Your users, tests, questions, certificates, and forms all belong to one organization.

Most deployments have one organization per institution — for example, a single state government, a non-profit, or a training partner. Larger deployments may host many organizations on the same Sashakt platform, each with its own admins and data.

:::tip
You won't usually create or switch organizations as part of day-to-day work — your organization is set up once at the platform level.
:::

## Question

A **question** is one item in your question bank. Sashakt supports several question types:

- **Single-choice** — Pick one option
- **Multi-choice** — Pick one or more options
- **Subjective** — Free-text answer with an optional character limit
- **Numerical** — Integer or decimal answer
- **Matrix** — A grid (rating, matching, or input)

A question can include **images** (uploaded directly) or **audio and video** (referenced by URL), support **partial marking**, and carry **tags** to make it easy to find later.

## Question Bank

The **question bank** is your library of questions. You add questions to it once, then reuse them across many tests.

Question banks are managed at the organization level. If your role is scoped (e.g. State Admin), you'll only see questions visible to your scope.

## Test

A **test** is a specific assessment delivered to candidates. A test is built by picking questions from the question bank and configuring how the test behaves:

- **Time limit** — How long candidates have to finish
- **Sections** — Group questions into logical chunks
- **Scoring** — Marks per question, negative marking, partial marks
- **OMR mode** — Bubble-sheet style answering as an alternative to per-question cards
- **Feedback** — Whether candidates see their answers after submitting

Every test gets a unique link. That link is what you share with candidates.

## Tag

A **tag** is a label you attach to questions or tests to organize them — for example, _Mathematics_, _Class 8_, _2024 Batch_. Tags make large question banks searchable and let you build tests by topic instead of by hand-picking questions.

## Form

A **form** is what candidates fill in before they start a test — their name, district, school, phone number, or anything else you need. Forms are built block by block in the Portal and support many field types (text, dropdown, date, location search, multi-select, and more).

You can attach the same form to many tests, or build a custom form for a specific test.

## Entity

An **entity** represents a real-world object that your organization manages or works with. Each organization can define its own entity types based on its needs—for example, Schools, Hospitals, Anganwadi Centres, Wards, Training Centres, or SHGs (Self-Help Groups). These entities can then be associated with users, tests, forms, or other records within Sashakt.

You'll see entities show up inside forms (as dropdowns and search fields) and inside roles (when scoping a State Admin to a state, or a Test Admin to a state or district).

## Certificate

A **certificate** is a document issued to a candidate after they complete (or pass) a test. You design the look in **Google Slides** — add your logo, layout, and signatures — and drop in **tokens** like `{{candidate_name}}` and `{{score}}` where personalized values should appear. Sashakt fills in each candidate's values and generates the PDF.

## Candidate

A **candidate** is the person taking a test. Candidates don't log in to the Portal — they use the **Sashakt Webapp** with a test link, fill in the registration form, and take the test.

## Test Attempt

A **test attempt** is an instance of a candidate attempting a test, irrespective of whether the candidate completes and submits the test or not.

## How these fit together

Here's the typical flow, in plain language:

1. You build a **question bank**.
2. You group questions with **tags**.
3. You design a **form** to capture candidate info.
4. You create a **test** — pick questions, attach the form, set the rules.
5. You share the test link. **Candidates** register through the form and take the test.
6. Sashakt scores submissions and optionally issues **certificates** based on the test configuration.

The rest of this documentation walks through each of those steps in detail.
